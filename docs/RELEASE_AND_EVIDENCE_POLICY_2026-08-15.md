# DGAF Release and Evidence Policy — 2026-08-15

## Current posture

DGAF is a governance/research framework with substantial implementation and historical process artifacts. It also contains active draft material, including STASIS Cluster 1 work. Framework terminology, project-local gates, attestations, and stewardship metadata must not be represented as independent scientific certification or external authority.

## Versioning assessment

DGAF contains existing v3.x component/version terminology. Preserve component-local versions where they identify actual implementation or schema revisions. Do not infer that a component version is a framework-wide validation level.

Use explicit draft labels (`draft`, `proposal`, `experimental`) for work that has not completed its stated evidence gates. Historical milestones remain historical after their dates pass; they should not be silently presented as active deadlines.

## Evidence rule

A governance mechanism may be:

- specified;
- implemented;
- tested;
- internally attested;
- externally sourced;

without those states being equivalent.

Project-local stewardship and rubric scores do not constitute external certification. Mathematical/formal claims require derivation or appropriate source support. Empirical claims require reproducible evidence.

## Current open material

PRs and drafts that explicitly identify themselves as non-canonical or project-local should retain that status until their merge gates and evidence requirements are actually satisfied. In particular, historical migration deadlines must be reconciled to current state before publication.
