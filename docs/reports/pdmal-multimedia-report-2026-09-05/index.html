PDMALPhi-Driven Multi-Agent Lattice

PDMAL is the part of DGAF being developed to test how a governed multi-agent system behaves when its communication structure changes and failures are introduced.

PDMAL is DGAF's topology-focused experimental substrate for deterministic ConsensusTask execution across a preregistered topology × condition × failure matrix with fail-closed TGL treatment semantics and provenance-bound evidence.

Fail-closed behavior

A required gate that is not wired is not an implicit pass. It yields SKIP, escalates the turn, and prevents silent proxy substitution.

Interpretation

PDMAL is becoming a testable scientific instrument, not merely a topology idea. Its governance exists to prevent engineering progress from being mislabeled as experimental proof.

The evidentiary harness—candidate binding, deterministic artifacts, blinding, custody, analysis locking, non-transfer rules, and fail-closed semantics—is part of the feature's engineering contribution.

Bottom line

Engineering maturity: substantial. Experimental maturity: pre-freeze. Authorization: not granted. Empirical claim strength: none yet; N=0 by design.
