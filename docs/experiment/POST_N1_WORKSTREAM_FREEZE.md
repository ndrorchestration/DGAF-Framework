# Post-N=1 Workstream Freeze

**Scope:** bounded period surrounding the first N=1 operational characterization

The following workstreams are paused as prerequisites to execution. They may resume after the N=1 result is preserved and classified, or earlier only if a documented blocking defect requires narrowly scoped repair.

- Phi-calculus/formal-semantics refinement
- grant prospecting and grant narrative refinement
- README/public-facing architecture polishing
- exhaustive comparative-baseline research
- new governance or provenance layers not required for interpretability
- expansion of the empirical sample before the N=1 characterization is recorded

## Exception rule

A workstream may interrupt the freeze only when it addresses a concrete defect that blocks the defined N=1 observation. The intervention must identify the blocking predicate, change only what is necessary, and trigger candidate re-identification/re-verification where the apparatus identity changes.

## Principle

The existence of additional desirable rigor is not, by itself, evidence that the first bounded operational observation should be postponed.
