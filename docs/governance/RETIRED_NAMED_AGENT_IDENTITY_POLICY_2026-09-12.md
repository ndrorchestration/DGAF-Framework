# Retired Named-Agent Identity Policy — 2026-09-12

## Decision

A formerly named verification/evidence-governance persona is retired from current architecture and product language.

Its useful responsibilities remain available as **functional roles**, including:

- source validation;
- bounded quality-gate review;
- evidence provenance checks;
- integrity scoring;
- rubric application;
- dissent/error escalation;
- evidence-governance support;
- final-verification preparation where separately authorized by current governance.

The historical proper name may appear only when necessary to preserve or explain:

- event-time provenance;
- immutable or hash-bound evidence;
- historical protocol identity;
- compatibility identifiers;
- migration lineage;
- old filenames, paths, commits, issues, or artifacts that cannot be safely rewritten without damaging traceability.

## Current-use prohibition

Do not present the retired name as:

- a current agent identity;
- a current authority source;
- a product or feature name;
- a certification actor;
- an autonomous final decision-maker;
- a marketing persona;
- a current governance status issuer.

Current documentation should refer to the surviving capabilities by function rather than persona name.

## Historical DGAF records

Files, gates, experiments, validators, and evidence records containing the former name are **historical identifiers** unless a current canonical specification explicitly re-establishes their mechanics under a functional role. Their existence does not reactivate the retired identity or transfer historical authority into the present control state.

This policy does not rewrite signed, hashed, evidence-bound, or otherwise provenance-sensitive historical artifacts. Those records remain intact and are interpreted through this policy.

## Governance state

This naming decision does not alter DGAF authorization state, freeze state, empirical state, gate results, or experiment permissions. It is a vocabulary/identity migration only.
