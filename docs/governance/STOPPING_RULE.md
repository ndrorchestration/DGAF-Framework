# DGAF/PDMAL Control Addition Stopping Rule

A new blocking control may be added only when all of the following are true:

1. A concrete failure mode or material uncertainty has been identified.
2. Failure could materially affect experimental validity, evidence integrity, reproducibility, security, blinding, or authorization.
3. The risk is not already covered by an existing predicate/control.
4. The proposed control produces new protective or evidentiary information rather than duplicating an existing check.

Once a material risk is covered, additional controls are non-blocking unless a new failure mode is identified.

## Evidence principle

Evidence should become stronger, not merely larger.

## Non-authority rule

Checklists, predicate inventories, status files, and declarations do not make a predicate true. Closure must be derived from the underlying authoritative evidence and, where required, independent verification.

## Scope

This rule applies to the PDMAL pre-freeze and pilot-readiness control plane. It does not prohibit ordinary engineering tests, maintenance, or non-blocking research improvements; it prevents those activities from silently becoming new pilot-blocking requirements.
