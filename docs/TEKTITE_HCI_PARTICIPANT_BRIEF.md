# Tektite First-Time User Study — Participant Brief

Thank you for helping test an experimental governance interface.

## What you need

Use this exact website:

[Tektite participant surface](https://dynamicgovernanceagenticformation-kol94uquc-ndrorchestration.vercel.app/demo)

You do **not** need to install anything, create an account, or understand DGAF/Tektite beforehand.

## Important

Please do not ask the facilitator what the interface is supposed to mean while you are completing the tasks. If something is confusing, that confusion is useful evidence.

There are no trick questions. We are testing the interface, not you.

## Tasks

While using the site, answer these in your own words:

1. What do you think this system is showing you?
2. What is the current state of the item or system you are viewing?
3. What evidence appears to support that state?
4. What appears to be the most important blocker or unmet condition?
5. Is the system ready, authorized, both, or neither for the visible next action? Why?
6. Where would you go to inspect more detail before acting?
7. What next action would you try?
8. What happened when you tried it?
9. If an action is blocked or unavailable, what do you think must change before it should become available?
10. What felt misleading, hidden, redundant, or unnecessarily difficult?

## Privacy

The study record should use a random participant ID rather than your name. Do not include passwords, private account data, medical information, or other sensitive personal information.

## What this study does not do

Your participation does not authorize any action in the system and does not constitute scientific or security validation of DGAF. The study is limited to whether the interface communicates state, evidence, blockers, and action boundaries clearly.
