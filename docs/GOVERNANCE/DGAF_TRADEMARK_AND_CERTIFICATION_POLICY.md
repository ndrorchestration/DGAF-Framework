# DGAF Trademark & Certification Policy

**Status:** Proposed governance policy; no active certification program
**Date:** 2026-08-25

## Purpose

Separate software licensing from use of DGAF names, marks, endorsement language, and any future certification or assessment program.

## Current legal boundary

The DGAF repository is currently licensed under **Apache License 2.0**. The license grants the software permissions stated in the license but does not grant permission to use the licensor's trade names, trademarks, service marks, or product names.

This policy does not create a trademark right merely by declaration. Actual trademark ownership, registration, and enforcement depend on applicable law and should be confirmed through appropriate legal counsel before a commercial program is launched.

## Current status

There is **no active DGAF certification program** established by this document.

The following must not be represented as currently official merely because the repository is public, a pattern is registered, a project agent attested it, or a test was run:

- DGAF Certified
- DGAF Official
- DGAF Endorsed
- DGAF Verified
- DGAF Approved
- Guaranteed DGAF compliant

Project-local terms may be used descriptively when they do not imply official certification, endorsement, or independent accreditation.

## Future certification requirements

Before any certification/assessment program becomes active, it should define, in public and versioned form:

1. **Scope** — what exactly is being assessed.
2. **Criteria** — objective pass/fail or graded requirements.
3. **Version binding** — framework/pattern/schema version, commit, or release tested.
4. **Evidence** — reproducible records sufficient to substantiate the result.
5. **Independence** — who performs assessment and what conflicts of interest exist.
6. **Expiration** — how long an assessment remains valid.
7. **Revocation** — conditions that invalidate a prior result.
8. **Appeal/review** — mechanism for challenging an assessment.
9. **Limitations** — what the certification explicitly does not establish.
10. **Non-guarantees** — explicit separation from universal safety, correctness, security, or business-outcome guarantees.

## Epistemic boundary

Certification is an assessment result, not proof that every underlying scientific or engineering claim is true. A certified artifact could satisfy a defined conformance profile while still containing unvalidated assumptions outside that profile.

Conversely, a technically validated research result does not automatically authorize use of DGAF certification terminology.

## Relationship to open source

Open-source licensing and certification are intentionally separate:

```text
Apache-2.0 software rights
        ≠
official DGAF endorsement
        ≠
DGAF certification
```

Users may exercise the rights granted by the license. Official marks or certification claims require separate governance.

## Relationship to Pattern Commons

Pattern Commons records provenance, semantic relationships, evidence, and epistemic state. A pattern's presence in Pattern Commons does not confer certification status.

Commercial ownership and trademark status must likewise remain distinct from epistemic status.

## Non-guarantee

Nothing in this policy establishes that DGAF, any pattern, any implementation, or any future certification program guarantees safety, correctness, regulatory compliance, security, reliability, business performance, or fitness for a particular purpose outside the expressly defined assessment scope.
