# DGAF External Evidence Policy

External sources are categorized as methodology, benchmark, background, or independent evidence.

Methodology and background sources inform definitions and experiment design. They do not establish DGAF performance. Benchmark results from another system do not transfer automatically to DGAF. Independent evidence may support a claim only when construct, population/context, intervention, outcome, and measurement are sufficiently aligned and provenance is auditable.

All imported evidence must preserve source identity, date/version, scope, limitations, and the exact DGAF claim it is proposed to support.
