# H4 / Topology Lab Evidence Status

**Classification:** SYNTHETIC / HISTORICAL

The retained H4 / topology-lab outputs associated with `TRIAD-EXP-001` are historical synthetic artifacts. They are **not empirical efficacy evidence** and must not be used to advance DGAF efficacy, authorization, independent-validation, High-Assurance, or scientific-N state.

The historical manifest declared **2,520** runs, but the repository retains only **80 retained data rows** in `h4_raw_results.csv`. The full historical raw population is therefore not retained and cannot be independently reconstructed or verified from the currently retained evidence.

The historical manifest named `pptl/experiments/h4_task_stratified.py` as its runner. That **runner binding is contradicted** by the retained implementation: the named simulator has a different experiment shape and cannot generate the manifest's declared 2,520-run design. No replacement generator has been verified. The historical runner claim is preserved for provenance rather than treated as a valid current binding.

Historical values and labels such as `CONFIRMED`, `+0.117`, topology-superiority, and noise-resilience statements remain preserved as historical synthetic outputs only. They must not be promoted as empirical efficacy findings without a separately authorized, provenance-complete empirical experiment.
